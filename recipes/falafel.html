Falafel

Description

Falafel is a deep-fried ball or patty made from ground chickpeas, fava beans, or both. It is a popular Middle Eastern food.

It is often served in a pita bread with salad and tahini sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Soak the chickpeas in water for at least 2 hours.
  2. Drain the chickpeas and add them to a food processor with the onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper.
  3. Process until the mixture is finely chopped but not smooth.
  4. Shape the mixture into balls or patties.
  5. Heat oil in a deep skillet over medium heat.
  6. Fry the falafel until golden brown on all sides.
